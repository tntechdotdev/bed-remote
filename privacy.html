Privacy Policy

Bed Remote for KSBT Beds · effective November 17, 2026

Bed Remote is a Bluetooth remote control for adjustable bed bases that use a Keeson KSBT controller. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no internet permission, so it cannot send information off your phone.

Information stored on your phone

To reconnect to your bed, the app saves a few settings in its own storage on your phone:

  • the Bluetooth name and address of the bed controller(s) you chose,
  • whether you left the connection on or off, whether your bed is split and which side you control,
  • whether vibration on button presses is on,
  • the date you first opened the app, the last day it showed the unlock reminder, and whether you've unlocked it.

These settings never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Bluetooth and the "Nearby devices" permission

The app asks Android for the Nearby devices permission so it can find and connect to your bed's Bluetooth controller. It only lists devices whose name shows they are bed controllers, it only talks to the controller you choose, and it declares to Android that it never uses Bluetooth scans to work out your location. On Android 11 and older, Android requires location permission for any Bluetooth scan. The app asks for it there only for that reason and never reads your location.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account owns the unlock. The developer receives no payment details from the app.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer any information to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
